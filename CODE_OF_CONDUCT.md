# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MagicForge a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and care for the privacy and intellectual property of researchers and performers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, publishing another person's private information, exposing restricted magic methods or private corpus material, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Until a formal conduct contact is published, report incidents privately through the official repository's GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting channel. Do not place personal or sensitive details in a public issue.
