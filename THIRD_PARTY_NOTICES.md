# Third-party notices

MagicForge depends on third-party Python and JavaScript packages. Their names
and requested versions are declared in `requirements.txt` and
`frontend/package-lock.json`. Each dependency remains subject to its own
license and notice requirements.

This file is a boundary notice, not a generated bill of materials and not a
substitute for dependency-license review. Before a binary, container, or hosted
release, generate an SBOM from the resolved dependency lock, collect required
license texts and notices, and review copyleft, attribution, font, model, and
SDK terms for the exact shipped versions.

In particular, model weights and runtime-downloaded embedding assets are not
licensed by the MagicForge Apache-2.0 license. The operator must review and
accept the applicable model and provider terms before distribution or use.
